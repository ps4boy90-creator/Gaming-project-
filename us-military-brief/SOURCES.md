conservative, officially-reported figure and rounds it ("~", "over", "nearly").

## Deliberate wording choices

- **"Tens of thousands of objects"** rather than a single number: the public catalog
  count (50,000+, including debris) and the ">10 cm tracked object" count (~29,000)
  differ enough that a specific figure would be misleading.
- **"~$1 trillion"** rather than a precise total: FY2026 national defense funding
  arrives through two separate vehicles and totals are quoted differently.
- **"~300 warships"** uses the Navy's battle-force definition, not the smaller
  "active commissioned hulls" count some trackers publish.
